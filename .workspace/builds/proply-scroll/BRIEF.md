# PROPLY — Scroll-Driven Product Experience

## Interview Answers (Verbatim)

### 1. Vibe
Futuristic, cinematic, precise, premium.

References:
- Apple product launch films
- Blade Runner 2049
- Nothing Phone product design

### 2. Scroll Journey
At first the visitor should not immediately understand everything about Proply.
They should first feel that they are entering a highly polished digital
product experience.

Then a premium laptop/product object appears.

As the visitor scrolls, the laptop moves closer and changes camera angles.
The real Proply website UI is visible on the laptop screen.

As scrolling continues, the interface on the laptop changes naturally,
showing different parts of the product.

The laptop eventually pulls back and the experience explains what Proply
actually does for businesses.

The final section becomes extremely clean and confident, ending with a
strong CTA.

### 3. Energy Curve
Quiet → Curious → Rising → Peak → Calm → Confident

The main visual peak should be the laptop + real Proply interface reveal.

### 4. Feeling + One Moment
Curiosity → fascination → immersion → confidence.

The one moment people should remember:
"The website inside the laptop actually feels alive and changes as I scroll."

### 5. Difference
The website should behave like a self-demonstrating digital product.

Instead of a traditional SaaS landing page, Proply should demonstrate the
quality of its web design through the interaction and visual storytelling
of the website itself.

### 6. Premium-Minimal Scale
Premium-minimal + cinematic + editorial.

Minimal but not empty.
Large typography.
Strong whitespace.
High-quality product imagery.
Controlled motion.
Subtle depth and lighting.

Avoid:
- generic SaaS cards
- purple AI gradients
- excessive glassmorphism
- generic dashboards
- repetitive feature grids
- unnecessary UI decoration

### 7. World or Scenes
One continuous world.

The laptop, camera and interface should feel like they exist in the same
physical environment.

Prefer continuous camera movement and transitions over disconnected
sections.

### 8. Existing Assets
Proply logo and existing brand identity are available.

Use the real Proply website UI inside the laptop.

A premium 3D laptop model can be created or sourced.

Missing visual/video assets can be generated.

The laptop screen must look like a real working Proply website, not a fake
generic dashboard.

## The Peak
Act 3 — Reveal. The moment the laptop screen lights up with the real Proply website. This is the largest visual change, the most scroll room, and the silence before it (Act 2) is quieter.

## Tell-Someone Sentence
"It's the site where a laptop appears from the dark and the website inside it actually changes as you scroll."

## Journey (6 Beats)

1. **Darkness** — Abstract, no context. Just light and texture.
2. **Approach** — Something is there. Camera moves toward it.
3. **Reveal** — The laptop. Full product. Screen on.
4. **Immersion** — The UI on the screen changes with scroll. The product demonstrates itself.
5. **Context** — What is Proply? Why does this matter?
6. **Close** — One action. Clean. Confident.

## Grammar Choice

**Filmic one-shot** — continuous camera movement, no hard cuts, the laptop exists in one physical space.

Why not the other seven:
- Chaptered editorial: breaks the continuous world requirement
- Live surface: too chaotic for premium-minimal
- Typographic poster: wrong for product showcase
- Gallery: wrong structure
- Split stage: breaks continuity
- Rhythmic cutlist: too choppy
- Continuous world: this IS the continuous world grammar (subset of filmic)

## Signature Move

**Screen-synced scroll scrub** — as the visitor scrolls, the laptop screen content changes in sync. The scroll position maps to the screen state. Not a video playing — real DOM elements on the screen that respond to scroll position. The laptop screen IS the scroll progress indicator.
